Sulfolobus acidocaldarius Transcriptomic Atlas

Brück et al., 2025 · A multi-omics resource for RNA regulation

This interactive platform integrates dRNA-seq, Term-seq, RIP-seq, and genome annotation data to provide a comprehensive view of post-transcriptional regulation in the thermoacidophilic archaeon S. acidocaldarius. Explore gene expression dynamics, asRNA/mRNA pairs, processing site motifs, and functional arCOG mapping.
